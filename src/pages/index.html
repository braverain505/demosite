Our shared purpose

“Every child who walks through our gates learns to succeed — academically, morally and socially.”

That is the Excellence promise. We balance the best of the British and Nigerian curricula across four key stages, taught by subject specialists in small, supported groups — with strong safeguarding, a house system and a moral and citizenship core at the centre of everything.

Why EIS

A school built around your child’s success

Our founder’s vision — and the promise we operate by — is simple: every child can learn and succeed. Everything on this campus is arranged around that idea.

  • British & Nigerian curricula balanced across Key Stages 1–4
  • Three fully-equipped science laboratories, used from Primary
  • A caring boarding house — “a home away from home” — with dining halls and evening study
  • Strong pastoral care, prefect system and a parents’ forum that really meets
  • Safe, disciplined atmosphere on a purpose-built campus in Jalingo
