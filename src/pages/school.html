Our story

A school that opened its gates on 19 September 2016

Excellence International Schools opened in Jalingo, Taraba State, on 19 September 2016, a Saturday schoolroom with a conviction too large for its size: everybody can learn and succeed.

Ten years on, that conviction still runs the school. We balance the British and Nigerian curricula across Key Stages 1 to 4, from Nursery & Early Years to Senior Secondary, with strong safeguarding, disciplined habits and a warm, structured daily life.

This overview brings together the school’s academic, pastoral and community priorities.

Mission & vision

What we promise, in our own words

Our mission

“Everybody can learn and succeed. Our shared purpose is that every child who walks through our gates learns to succeed, academically, morally and socially.”

Our vision

“To create a Key Stages 1 to 4 school that balances the British and Nigerian curricula: a school every parent in Taraba is proud to send their child to, and where every child reaches God given potential.”
