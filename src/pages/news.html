A decade of Excellence — EIS turns 10

Founded on 19 September 2016, EIS has grown from one Jalingo classroom to a full Key Stages 1–4 school. This September we mark the 10th anniversary with a reunion week for alumni, families and friends — assemblies, old photographs, and the prize-giving that has closed every year since the first.

The anniversary itself is real; details of the reunion week are filled in by the office at launch.
