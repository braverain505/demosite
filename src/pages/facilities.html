Facility · Science

Three laboratories and a theory that works

Biology, chemistry and physics each have their own laboratory at EIS — and they are not reserved for the senior years. Primary pupils use the labs, so “science” stops being a rumour and starts being a bench, a flame and a question.

The annual science fair turns thirty-plus exhibits of pupil work into the best evening of the school year.

Facilities · Reading

A library that takes young readers seriously

The reading room is one of the quietest places on campus for a reason — it is one of the busiest. Shelf space grows with every term, quiet corners are fought over at break, and the librarian knows exactly what each age group will love next.

Reading runs through the timetable, not beside it: DEAR (drop everything and read) time appears in every week.

Facilities · Sport & games

Fields, goals and inter-house pride

There is football at the heart of the campus, athletics across the field, and a reliable supply of inter-house rivalry around sports day. But sport at EIS is bigger than the ball: it is where persistence, teamwork and losing-with-grace get taught.

Facilities · Boarding

The boarding house: a home away from home

For Junior and Senior boarders, the boarding house is arranged around care. Rooms are clean and calm; evenings run on a settled routine of supervised prep, supper in the hall and lights-out handled with kindness.

  • Dedicated pastoral staff who know every boarder by name
  • Evening prep supervised, homework-checked before lights-out
  • Two dining halls, hot supper, and weekends home
