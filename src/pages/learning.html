From Nursery to Senior Secondary — the British curriculum’s key-stage structure meets the Nigerian curriculum readers, parents and the exam boards expect.

The four key stages

Choose a stage to see what your child will meet

Nursery & Early Years · Ages 2–5

A warm, language-rich start where play and structure meet. Early literacy, numeracy and social skills are seeded in small, cared-for groups — so the first school years are full of curiosity, not anxiety.

  • Early literacy and number work through play-based learning
  • Speech, social skills and kindness routines modelled daily
  • Gentle, positive transitions — including the first school trips
  • Close communication between class teacher and home

Ages and framing match the school’s advertised bands; exact class groups are set by the admissions and academic offices.

Primary · Key Stage 1–2 · Ages 5–11

This is where the fundamentals are truly mastered — reading with fluency, writing with confidence, mathematics with number sense, and science with hands on the apparatus, because laboratory work begins in Primary at EIS.

  • English, Mathematics, Science and a full foundation curriculum
  • Science laboratories used from Primary onwards
  • Clubs begin: reading, chess, art and more
  • House membership and early leadership responsibilities

Junior Secondary · Key Stage 3 · Ages 11–14

The broad bridge to the senior years. Subject departments get real, the three laboratories get busy, and every pupil carries growing responsibility in school life.

  • Real subject departments — sciences, humanities, languages
  • Regular laboratory practice across biology, chemistry, physics
  • School Parliament, prefects-in-training, inter-house competition
  • Team sports and the start of exam-hall literacy

Senior Secondary · Key Stage 4+ · Ages 14–18

The final ascent to the exams that matter. Subject specialists, exam-hall practice and a firm pathway to university — with character and leadership still part of the timetable.

  • WAEC/NECO preparation and exam-training discipline
  • Science options across the three laboratories
  • University pathway: choices, applications, confidence
  • Prefectcy, house captaincy and mentor circles

The Admissions Office can confirm current examination pathways and subject options.

Science & STEM

Three laboratories, kindled from Primary

Biology, chemistry and physics are not a senior-school afterthought here. From Primary onwards, pupils wear the goggles and take off the lid — and the annual science fair gives every stage a stage.

  • Three dedicated laboratories for biology, chemistry and physics
  • Practical work from Primary upwards, not just demonstration
  • A science fair with 30+ exhibits across Key Stages
  • Safe-school rules and lab discipline taught early
