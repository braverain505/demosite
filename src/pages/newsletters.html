EIS Autumn 2026 · Term 1

Resumption Newsletter

Excellence International Schools, Jalingo — 2026–2027 Academic Session

Esteemed Parents,

It is with great pleasure that I welcome you to the 2026–2027 Academic Session. I trust you and your family enjoyed a refreshing and memorable summer holiday. We are delighted to have our learners back in school and are especially excited to welcome the new families who have joined the Excellence International School community.

This academic year, our theme is “Excellence Without Limits.” It reflects our commitment to nurturing every learner to achieve their highest potential academically, socially, morally and emotionally. Our dedicated staff are enthusiastic and fully prepared for another rewarding year of learning, growth and innovation. Their work began well before resumption and we sincerely appreciate their unwavering commitment in ensuring that our school environment is well-organised, welcoming and ready to provide exceptional learning experiences from the very first day.

During the first few weeks of the term, our focus will be on Scaffolding for Learning, an intentional approach that helps learners build strong foundations by introducing concepts in manageable stages while strengthening their understanding and confidence. In our Early Years Foundation Stage, particular emphasis will be placed on “Learning through play,” where learners will become familiar with classroom routines, school expectations, positive behaviour and the values that will guide their learning journey throughout the year. Establishing these routines early is essential in creating a safe, structured and engaging environment where every child can thrive.

As part of our continuous pursuit of excellence, we have introduced differentiated learning strategies designed to meet the needs of learners at different ability levels. This approach ensures that every child receives the appropriate support and challenge required for meaningful academic progress. We firmly believe that education is most effective when there is a strong partnership between the home and the school. With your continued support and collaboration, we are confident that our learners will experience remarkable success throughout the session.

To further strengthen the quality of teaching and learning, our academic and non-academic staff participated in several days of intensive professional development before resumption. These training sessions were facilitated both internally and in collaboration with medical personnel. As a certified Cambridge International School, our secondary learners will once again participate in the prestigious Cambridge Checkpoint Examinations this November, alongside other enriching academic and co-curricular programmes that promote critical thinking, leadership, creativity and global citizenship.

We are optimistic that this session will be one of our most productive and inspiring yet. We sincerely appreciate your trust in Excellence International School and remain committed to providing an educational experience that prepares every learner for success in an ever-changing world. We also value your ideas and suggestions, as our doors remain open to constructive partnerships that will continue to move our school forward.

To our new parents, welcome to the Excellence family. We encourage you to provide your WhatsApp number to the Vice Principal (Administration) so that you can be added to our official Parents’ WhatsApp Forum, where important updates and school information are regularly shared. Kindly note that we will also continue to communicate through our communication books, emails and official social media platforms to ensure you remain well informed.

Finally, we wish to remind all parents that from the second week of resumption, the school gate will be closed at 7:45 a.m. from Monday to Friday. This long-standing policy is aimed at encouraging punctuality and helping our learners develop the discipline necessary for academic excellence.

We kindly ask that you familiarise yourself with the school calendar and other important information for the term to ensure a smooth and successful school year.

Thank you for your continued confidence in Excellence International School. We look forward to partnering with you in making this academic session a truly outstanding one. Together, let us pursue Excellence Without Limits.

You are valued,

Dr. Jeremiah Friday Principal
