What you need

Everything to prepare before you start

A completed application form, the child’s birth certificate and two passport size photographs. For transfers: the most recent school reports, plus any exam slips for older students.

Nursery & Early Years from age 2; Primary (KS1 to KS2) from age 5; Junior Secondary (KS3) from age 11; Senior Secondary (KS4+) from age 14. Class placement is confirmed by the academic office after assessment.

A short age appropriate assessment and an interview with the Admissions team. It is about fit, not a pass/fail gate. We find the right class for the child, not the other way round.

Boarding is available for Junior and Senior pupils. Families applying for boarding also share a brief note on the child’s routine and any medical needs. The house staff read it before day one.

We accept enquiries all year. For the 2026/27 session, we recommend applying before the term opens on 14 September 2026. Term dates are shared with families by the school office.
